Points

Tap HIT once per shot as the rally happens (optional — you can skip straight to the outcome), then tap Point → for whoever won it. A 1-hit point is an ace/double fault, 2 hits is a return winner/error, and so on — the description is derived, never typed. If the first serve is out, tap 1st serve out instead of an outcome button — it discards that serve and waits for the second; tap it again if the second serve is out too and the point ends itself as a double fault. Break/set/match points are tagged automatically and feed the Players tab's fatigue and clutch breakdowns.

On a keyboard: A or S / L or ; log a hit (the same player can't hit twice in a row — a rally always alternates). QWER ends the point for the left-hand player, UIOP ends it for the right-hand player. F marks a serve fault, T discards a let/replay without scoring it, and B (or the "mark ✕" button) drops a ✕ into the Rally track as a plain visual note — it doesn't count as a hit or change the description.
